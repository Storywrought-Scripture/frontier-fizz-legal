Delete your Frontier Fizz data

Frontier Fizz by Serna Studios

How to request deletion

  1. Open Frontier Fizz, tap The Wall, then Settings, and note your leaderboard name (for example "Dusty Comet 4821").
  2. Email andrewserna@gmail.com with the subject "Frontier Fizz data deletion" and include your leaderboard name.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  4. To remove everything stored on your phone, uninstall the game (or clear its data in Android settings).

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous Unity player ID and its leaderboard entries (your leaderboard name and scores).
  • Everything the game stores on your device (progress, scrip, unlocks, settings), when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing that we control is kept after a deletion request.
  • Your "Remove ads" purchase is recorded by Google Play, not by us, and stays with your Google account so you can restore it.
  • Information that Google AdMob collected to show ads is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

More detail is in the Frontier Fizz Privacy Policy.
