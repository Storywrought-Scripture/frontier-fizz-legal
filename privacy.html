Frontier Fizz Privacy Policy

Serna Studios · Effective November 14, 2026

The short version. Frontier Fizz has no accounts, no chat and no typed names. Your progress stays on your device. The game uses Google AdMob to show ads, Google Play for the optional "Remove ads" purchase, and Unity Gaming Services for the daily and weekly leaderboards. Those services receive some technical information, described below. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Frontier Fizz ("the game") is made by Serna Studios ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email andrewserna@gmail.com.

Who the game is for

Frontier Fizz is a general-audience casual game for teens and adults. Its content is family-friendly, but it is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

What we do not collect

  • No account, real name, email address, phone number or password.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No chat, messages or other text that players type. Leaderboard names are generated by the game from a list of family-friendly words (for example "Dusty Comet 4821").

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: levels reached, scores, scrip (the in-game currency, which can only be earned by playing), unlocked items and looks, settings such as vibration, your generated leaderboard name, and whether you bought "Remove ads". This file stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information handled by the services we use

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google AdMob (ads) Your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. To show ads between runs and the optional "watch an ad to keep going" ad, measure them and prevent fraud.
Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Leaderboards) An anonymous player ID created by Unity, your generated leaderboard name, your daily and weekly scores, and technical information such as IP address and device type. To run the daily and weekly leaderboards. You are signed in anonymously; no account or personal details are needed.
Google Play (billing) Your purchase of "Remove ads". Google handles the payment; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made. To remove ads between runs and restore the purchase if you reinstall.

Each service handles information under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Ads and your choices

  • The game asks AdMob for non-personalised ads and limits ads to family-friendly content.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice later from The Wall → Settings → Privacy options, when that option is shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • "Remove ads" ($0.99) turns off the ads between runs. The "keep going" ad is always optional.

Leaderboards

Only your generated leaderboard name and your scores are shown to other players. The daily board resets every day and the weekly board every week. You can change your leaderboard name at any time from The Wall → Settings.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with the service providers listed above, so the game can work, show ads and run leaderboards. Under some US state laws, showing ads through an ad network can count as "sharing" for advertising; you can limit this with the choices in the "Ads and your choices" section.

How long information is kept

  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Leaderboard scores: shown until the daily or weekly reset; old boards are not archived.
  • Your anonymous Unity player ID: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Information held by Google: as described in Google's privacy policy.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, and to object to or limit some uses of it. To delete your leaderboard data, or for any other request, email andrewserna@gmail.com with your leaderboard name (shown in The Wall → Settings). We will respond within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.

Security

Information sent to Google and Unity travels over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the information we handle to the minimum the game needs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.
