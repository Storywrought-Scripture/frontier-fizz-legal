How it plays

A run takes a few minutes. A great one takes a lot longer.

Hold to pour

Press and hold anywhere to pour the syrup the customer ordered.

Let go on the line

Release inside the green band. A perfect stop earns more points and keeps your streak alive.

Serve and climb

Finish the order before patience runs out, then take on the next, faster level.
